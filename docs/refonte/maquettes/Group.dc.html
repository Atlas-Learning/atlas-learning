Mini-lesson · 12 min · English and Arabic

Equivalent fractions with the multiplication grid

1
Warm-up · 2 minShow two fraction bars. Which is bigger, 1/3 or 2/6? Most will say 2/6. Let them argue.
2
Model · 4 minFold a paper strip in thirds, then fold each third in two. Same shaded length, twice the pieces: multiply top and bottom by 2.
3
Guided practice · 4 min
1/4 = ?/8 2/3 = ?/12 3/5 = 9/? 1/2 = ?/10
4
Exit question · 2 minSolve 1/3 + 1/6 and explain the first step in one sentence.
